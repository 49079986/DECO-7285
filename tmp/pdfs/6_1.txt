Why are we doing this?
Steps:
1. Form a Team and Define a Project
   • No technology/app at this stage
2. Plan a study to investigate the problem further
   • E.g. “How do UQ’s International students manage
     their diet?”
      • Develop a suit of methods (interviews, observations, etc.)
        to find answer to the question
      • Engage with potential users and collect data
3. Analyse data and develop initial ideas for
   design
4. Develop prototype – Iteration 1
5. Engage with same users and trial/ evaluate/
   assess the prototype
6. Develop final prototype – Iteration 2
